Why this works

It is serious, easy to remember, and avoids the side project mascot problem. The orange dot is small enough to feel premium but strong enough to become a brand cue.

Usage rule

Use the orange dot only on the i. Do not add extra icons, faces, or mascot marks beside the wordmark unless the product later earns a separate mascot system.
